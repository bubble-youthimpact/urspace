This Privacy Policy explains what personal information URSPACE collects when you use urspacedesign.com, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. URSPACE is a student-led project based in the Republic of Korea, and we handle personal information in line with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

    Who we are

    URSPACE ("we", "us") runs urspacedesign.com, a platform for sharing sustainable design ideas for unused and abandoned spaces. If you have any question about this policy or your information, email us at admin.urspace@gmail.com.

    What we collect

    We only collect what we need for the feature you use.

    When youWe collectWhy
    Create an account with emailName, email address, passwordTo let you log in, comment on design sites and submit designs
    Continue with GoogleThe name and email address on your Google accountTo create and log in to your account
    Apply for the education programName, email address, phone number (optional)To review your application and contact you about the program
    Submit a designDesigner name, chosen site, design name, description, email address, phone number (optional), and the file you uploadTo review your design and contact you about it
    Comment on a design siteYour name and your commentTo show your comment on that site's page

    Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and stored in encrypted form. We never see it. When you continue with Google, Google also shares your profile picture with Firebase, but we don't use or display it.

    We don't use advertising or analytics tools, and we don't sell your personal information.

    What is shown publicly

    Comments you post on a design site page are visible to everyone, together with your name. You can delete your own comments at any time; only you can delete them. We won't publish a design you submit on the site without asking you first.

    Information stored in your browser

    The site stores a few small items in your browser, using local storage:

    • your language choice (English or Korean);
    • your login session, kept by Firebase so you stay logged in;
    • while a feature is still in preview, the information you enter into it, which stays only on your device.

    You can clear these at any time in your browser settings. Clearing them logs you out.

    Services that help us run the site

    We use the following services to provide the site. Some of them store or process data outside Korea.

    ServiceLocationWhat it receivesPurposeHow long
    Google LLC (Firebase Authentication)United StatesName, email address, encrypted password, sign-in recordsAccounts and loginUntil you delete your account
    Google LLC (Cloud Firestore)United StatesYour name and commentsStoring and showing comments on design site pagesUntil you or we delete the comment
    FormSubmit (formsubmit.co)United StatesThe details you enter in education applications and design submissions, and the file you uploadDelivering applications and designs to our email inboxAs set out in FormSubmit's policy
    Google LLC (Gmail)United StatesApplications and emails you send usOur email inboxAs described under "How long we keep it"

    Data is sent to these services over an encrypted connection at the moment you use the feature. When pages load, your browser also requests fonts from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, and the site's hosting provider receives standard technical information such as your IP address. These providers don't receive the information you type into the site.

    How long we keep it

    • Accounts: until you ask us to delete your account.
    • Education applications and design submissions: for up to one year after we finish reviewing them, then deleted, unless you take part in a program and we need them to support you.
    • Comments: until you or we remove them, or your account is deleted.

    If a law requires us to keep information for longer, we keep it only for that purpose and period.

    Information from Google

    When you continue with Google, we receive only your name and email address, and use them only to create and identify your URSPACE account. URSPACE's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

    Your rights

    You can ask us at any time to:

    • show you the personal information we hold about you;
    • correct information that is wrong;
    • delete your information or your account;
    • stop using your information for a particular purpose.

    Email admin.urspace@gmail.com from the address linked to your account or application. We'll reply within 10 days. A legal representative may also make these requests on your behalf.

    Children

    If you are under 14, a parent or legal guardian must agree before you create an account, apply for a program or submit a design. If we learn that we collected information from a child under 14 without that consent, we'll delete it.

    How we protect your information

    The site is served over HTTPS. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and never stored by us. Only the URSPACE team can access applications and submissions, and we limit that access to the people who review them.

    Person responsible for privacy

    Umin Lee, Founder and CEO
    Email: admin.urspace@gmail.com

    If you're not satisfied with how we handled a privacy concern, you can also contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, phone 118 in Korea).

    Changes to this policy

    If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also post a notice on the site.
